ReelCount · Your data

Delete your data

Everything ReelCount keeps is on your phone, so deleting it is in your hands. There’s no account to close, and you don’t need to ask us for anything.

Clear today’s count

  1. Open ReelCount and tap the settings button at the top of the home screen.
  2. Under Your data, tap Clear today’s count.

Today goes back to zero. Earlier days and your streak are kept.

Delete all history

  1. Open ReelCount and tap the settings button at the top of the home screen.
  2. Under Your data, tap Delete all history.
  3. Tap Delete it all to confirm.

Removes every day you’ve counted, your streak and the limits recorded for past days. It can’t be undone. The one-way fingerprints of clips seen today are cleared on their own at the end of the day.

Uninstall ReelCount

Uninstalling removes everything the app stored on your phone: counts, settings and fingerprints. Nothing is left behind anywhere else, because nothing was sent anywhere else.

Backed-up copies

If your phone backs up to your Google account, Android may include ReelCount’s data in that backup, as it does for most apps. Android backs up apps as part of the whole phone, so to remove it, turn off backup or delete that phone’s backup in your Google account settings. Google holds this backup, not us.

What we hold about you

Nothing from the app. This version of ReelCount doesn’t ask Android for internet access, so it can’t send us your counts or anything else, and there are no accounts.

If you’ve emailed us, we have that email. Ask and we’ll delete it, and confirm when it’s done: contact@livomind.com.

Stop counting, keep your history

Turn ReelCount off in Android Settings, under Accessibility. Counting stops straight away, and everything you’ve counted so far stays in the app until you delete it. Turn it back on whenever you like and it carries on where it left off.

Scroll Battles

Battles aren’t switched on yet, so nothing has been shared with anyone. When they arrive they’ll need an account, and this page will explain how to delete that account and everything attached to it, from inside the app and from here.

Full detail on what’s kept and why is in the privacy policy.
