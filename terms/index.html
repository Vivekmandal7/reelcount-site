Counting and accuracy

Today ReelCount counts short videos in Instagram, YouTube and Facebook, and it is learning to count Snapchat, TikTok, Moj, Josh, Snack Video and Kwai. Where an app is listed as still being learned, we don’t promise a count for it.

Counts can be wrong. When ReelCount can’t tell clips apart, it leaves them out rather than guessing, so it is more likely to count too few than too many. If one of those apps changes its layout, counting in it can stop until we release a fix. The app tells you when it notices counting has stopped.

Your phone’s own battery settings can also pause ReelCount in the background, which is common on Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Samsung and other phones. The app tells you when this happens and shows you which setting to change. The Help page has the steps for each make of phone.

Limits and blocking

If you turn blocking on, ReelCount covers the short-video player once you reach your daily limit, until the day ends. The rest of the app you’re in keeps working. Unless strict mode is on, you can take five more minutes.

We can’t promise blocking works on every phone, in every version of every app, or in every situation. Please don’t rely on ReelCount for anything where a block failing would cause real harm.
